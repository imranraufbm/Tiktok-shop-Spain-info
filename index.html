When you move to a 3PL (Spain)

3PL type What they charge for Key risk points
Low‑cost Iberia‑focused Storage, pick/pack, standard packaging, shipping per parcel, returns handling. Check minimum orders/day, onboarding fees, and rules for islands/oversize parcels.

Imports & customs: do you need a licence?
EORI number, customs and special cases
For most typical TikTok Shop products, you don’t need a special “import licence”, but you do need to handle customs correctly.

EORI and standard imports

  • 1
    To import goods from outside the EU into Spain, you generally need an EORI number, which identifies you with customs.
  • 2
    For most common consumer products there is no specific “import licence” beyond customs clearance, import VAT/duties and product compliance.
  • 3
    Many importers use freight forwarders or customs agents who handle declarations and advise on correct commodity codes and duty rates.

When special import licences or notifications apply

Certain categories of products require specific authorisations, registrations or stricter controls, for example:

  • 1
    Food, supplements and some cosmetics (health registrations, sanitary controls).
  • 2
    Medicines, medical devices, chemicals and hazardous substances.
  • 3
    Weapons, certain electronics with radio modules, and other controlled goods.

If you plan to sell anything beyond “standard” consumer items (e.g. nutrition, devices, chemical products), you should get specific customs and regulatory advice before importing or selling.

Operating from home – and from abroad
Spain‑based business, world‑based founders
Working from your flat in Spain
Lean setup
  • 1
    Register as autónomo and choose the right activity codes; use your flat as fiscal address.
  • 2
    No walk‑in clients and no noisy trucks: parcels come and go by courier, which is normally accepted as home‑based work.
  • 3
    When stock or parcel volume becomes too big at home, it’s your signal to move to a 3PL or warehouse.
Running the Spanish business from abroad
Plan residency & taxes
  • 1
    You can keep a Spanish autónomo or company while physically staying abroad, as long as your tax residency and treaties are respected.
  • 2
    Use online tools only: Spanish bank account, invoicing app, 3PL dashboard, and an online advisor to file taxes for you.
  • 3
    Before long‑term moves, get specific advice on tax residency to avoid double taxation or losing benefits.

VeriFactu & e‑invoicing – staying compliant automatically
VeriFactu is a system, not a new tax – it makes your invoices traceable and harder to alter.
What VeriFactu means
Anti‑fraud framework
  • 1
    Spain is rolling out certified invoicing software with QR codes and secure audit trail under “VeriFactu” rules.
  • 2
    Invoices can’t be quietly deleted or modified without leaving a trace, reducing fraud and disputes.
  • 3
    Over the next years, most autónomos and SMEs will need to use compatible software – better to start with it now.
How to make it work for you
Automation advantage
  • 1
    Pick an invoicing app that is ready for Spanish e‑invoicing and VeriFactu.
  • 2
    Connect your sales channels (TikTok Shop, others) and bank so data flows in automatically.
  • 3
    Give your advisor access so they can prepare VAT and income‑tax filings using real‑time data.

Step‑by‑step roadmap – from zero to scalable

  1. 1
